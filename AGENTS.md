# Agents

This repo is Shem Yu's resume source of truth.

- `career/**/*.md` — wiki and graph. Claim `text` is the locked fact.
- `views/` — which claims appear on which page. `views/detailed.yaml` is the full multi-page English resume. `views/one-pager.yaml` is the one-page version.
- `data/` — old YAML snapshot. Do not tailor from it and do not treat it as current.

Before producing, rewriting, or tailoring a resume, read [`skills/resume-tailoring/SKILL.md`](skills/resume-tailoring/SKILL.md) and [`RESUME_STANDARD.md`](RESUME_STANDARD.md). The skill is the method. The standard is the canonical publication gate.

Do not apply, submit, or send a resume on the owner's behalf.
